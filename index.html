Privacy policy

Publisher: Oleksandr Peryk

Privacy contact: carpe.diem.poland@gmail.com

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Applies to Lifetime Health 1.0, the local wellness app for iPhone.

Your health data stays in the app

Lifetime Health is a local wellness dashboard and diary. In this version, the developer does not receive your Apple Health readings, diary, nutrition entries, questions or photos through the app. The app has no account, advertising, tracking SDK, cloud sync or developer-hosted AI service.

Apple Health access is your choice

You choose which categories the app may read. The app keeps a protected local copy for history and calculations. It does not write, modify or delete your original Apple Health records. Access is optional; missing permissions or readings can make some features unavailable. Revoking access does not erase copies already retained in the app.

Local storage, removal and recovery

Your profile, logs, saved Ask replies and retained attachments are stored on your iPhone in protected storage. Local diary records and retained health caches are excluded from device backups. There is no cloud recovery or supported import/restore in this version.

You can delete individual local entries and clear saved Ask conversations. Removing the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local records. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you need those records. Your original Apple Health records are unaffected.

Exports are controlled by you

You can export a JSON copy of your local diary, referenced photos and saved Ask summaries. Complete imported Apple Health history is not included. The app does not encrypt the export. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read it. If you choose a cloud or sharing destination, that service receives the copy under its own practices. Export supports inspection and safekeeping; it cannot currently be imported to restore the app.

Ask and optional sensitive categories

On compatible devices, Ask can use Apple’s on-device model. Otherwise, it offers summaries from saved data. Questions and the selected saved context are processed locally; there is no developer-hosted cloud AI endpoint. Optional sensitive categories require their applicable opt-in. Turning an option off does not remove information from earlier saved replies. You can clear conversations from Ask’s options.

Camera and nutrition labels

Optional camera scanning recognizes nutrition labels on your iPhone. You review the portion, basis and values before saving. Manual entry is available. This version does not use cloud image recognition or estimate meal calories from a meal photo.

Support and this website

If you choose to email support, your email provider and the publisher’s email provider handle that message. The publisher receives what you send to respond to your request. Please do not include health records, diary exports or sensitive screenshots in an initial message.

This support website uses no application analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or contact forms. It is hosted using GitHub Pages and its hosting infrastructure. Hosting providers necessarily process technical request information to deliver and protect the website. The app’s local-data statements do not mean that a website visit or an email message leaves no technical record.

Wellness estimates

Recovery, Stress, Energy Reserve, Daily Rhythm, sleep quality and Biological Age are experimental app-calculated estimates. They are not validated medical measurements, diagnoses or predictions of an individual’s lifespan. Read the in-app inputs, coverage, methodology and limitations. Do not use these estimates to make medical decisions.

Questions and policy changes

For support or privacy questions, contact carpe.diem.poland@gmail.com. This policy must be updated if the product adds accounts, servers, analytics, cloud processing or another data destination.

Common questions

Why are some Apple Health readings missing?

Review the categories allowed for Lifetime Health in Apple Health, then check the app’s Settings → Import status for each metric. Apple Watch delivery may be delayed. Missing readings do not prove that no activity occurred or that a category’s read permission was granted.

How do I change my dashboard or appearance?

Open Settings to choose the appearance, preferred metrics and editable targets. Readings and experimental app estimates remain distinguishable in each metric’s detail screen.

How do nutrition-label scans work?

Scan the nutrition label, then confirm the portion, basis and recognized values before saving. Manual entry is available when scanning or camera permission is unavailable. Imported Apple Health nutrition and locally logged food are separate records.

Why is on-device AI unavailable?

Apple’s on-device model requires a compatible device and Apple Intelligence availability. Ask can still show summaries from saved data without that model. Check the label shown with each answer.

Can I back up or restore my diary?

You can export local diary records, referenced photos and saved Ask replies as JSON from Settings. Complete Apple Health history is excluded. Export is for inspection and safekeeping; import/restore and cloud recovery are not supported. The exported file is not encrypted by the app.

How can I remove saved data?

Delete individual local entries or clear saved conversations from Ask’s options. Removing the app removes its local records; offloading the app preserves app data. Removing Lifetime Health does not delete the original Apple Health records.

Do not delete the app to solve a problem unless you accept losing local records. There is no supported restore.

Are wellness scores medical results?

No. App-calculated scores, including Biological Age, are experimental wellness estimates. Open their detail screens to see the inputs, coverage, methodology and limitations. They are not diagnoses, medical measurements or individual lifespan predictions.

  3. Say whether it also happens offline or after reopening the app.
